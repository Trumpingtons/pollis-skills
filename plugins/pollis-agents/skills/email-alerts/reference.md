# The sources

For each source: who can use it, where its alerts are made, the search syntax that keeps them precise, and where the email goes. The sites change their pages; when a step does not match what the user sees, find the button with them.

## Google Scholar

- **Access**: free; a Google account.
- **Search syntax**: `"exact phrase"`, `allintitle: words` (every word in the title), `intitle:word`, `author:"J Smith"`, `source:"Journal of Econometrics"`, `-word` to exclude, `OR` in capitals. Example: `allintitle: forecasting "time series" -stock` or `"conformal prediction" source:"Journal of the American Statistical Association"`.
- **Create the alert**: at scholar.google.com, run the search; in the menu on the left (or at the bottom of the results on a narrow window), **Create alert**; enter the alerts address; **Create alert**. For an address that is not the Google account's, Google sends a confirmation email to it first.
- **Other alerts**: on an author's profile, **Follow** > new articles or new citations; at scholar.google.com/scholar_alerts, the alerts made, to edit or cancel.
- **Noise**: Scholar matches words anywhere in the full text. Prefer `allintitle:`, phrases and `source:`.

## JSTOR

- **Access**: a free personal JSTOR account (register at jstor.org); the articles need the user's institution or a JSTOR plan.
- **Alerts**: JSTOR's alerts are for new issues of journals: on a journal's page, the option to be told of new content (or from **My Workspace / account > Alerts**, where the account offers it). A saved search with an email may be offered on the results page.
- **Email**: to the account's address: sign up with the alerts address, or forward automatically.

## HeinOnline

- **Access**: through the user's institution (law schools, universities), with a free **MyHein** account made inside HeinOnline.
- **Search syntax**: fields such as `title:`, `creator:`, phrases in quotes, `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, proximity `"word1 word2"~10`.
- **Create the alert**: run the search, **Save Search** to MyHein, then turn on its email alert in **MyHein > Saved Searches**. For journals, **eTOC alerts** (electronic tables of contents) on a title's page.
- **Email**: to the address of the MyHein account, or one entered there.

## Nexis Uni

- **Access**: through the user's institution. Nexis Uni has no RSS for searches.
- **Search syntax**: `"exact phrase"`, connectors `and`, `or`, `and not`, proximity `w/5` (within 5 words), `w/p` (same paragraph), `w/s` (same sentence), `hlead(word)` (headline and lead paragraphs), `!` for word endings.
- **Create the alert**: run the search; the **alert** button (a bell) above the results; set the frequency and **Email** delivery with the alerts address.
- **Email**: to the address entered on the alert.

## SSRN

- **Access**: free account at ssrn.com.
- **Alerts**: **subscriptions to eJournals** (a subject network's new papers, by email), and following authors; the account's **My Subscriptions** lists them.
- **Search**: SSRN's alerts follow subjects and authors, not word searches: pick the narrowest eJournals.
- **Email**: to the account's address: sign up with the alerts address.

## Semantic Scholar

- **Access**: free account at semanticscholar.org.
- **Alerts**: on a paper's page, **Create Alert** for new citations of it; on an author's page, **Follow** for their new papers; research feeds that learn from papers the user marks relevant.
- **Email**: to the account's address: sign up with the alerts address.

## Web of Science

- **Access**: through the user's institution, signed in with a free Clarivate account.
- **Search syntax**: field tags `TI=` (title), `TS=` (topic: title, abstract and keywords), `AU=`, `SO=` (publication), `"exact phrase"`, `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, `NEAR/5`, `*` for word endings. Example: `TI=("time series" AND forecast*) NOT TS=stock*`.
- **Create the alert**: run the search; on the results page, **Create Alert**, named after the topic. On a record, **Create citation alert** for its new citations.
- **Email**: to the Clarivate account's address: sign up with the alerts address, or forward automatically.

## Scopus

- **Access**: through the user's institution, signed in with an Elsevier account.
- **Search syntax**: `TITLE( )`, `TITLE-ABS-KEY( )`, `AUTH( )`, `SRCTITLE( )`, `"exact phrase"`, `AND`, `OR`, `AND NOT`, `W/5`, `PRE/3`, `*` for word endings. Example: `TITLE-ABS-KEY("time series" W/3 forecast*) AND SRCTITLE("journal of forecasting")`.
- **Create the alert**: run the search; **Set search alert** above the results; name it after the topic and choose the frequency. Document citation alerts on an article's page, and author alerts on an author's.
- **Email**: to the Elsevier account's address unless the alert's form offers another: sign up with the alerts address, or forward automatically.

## Westlaw

- **Access**: through the user's law school or firm.
- **Search syntax**: terms and connectors: `"exact phrase"`, `&` (and), a space (or), `/s` (same sentence), `/p` (same paragraph), `/n` (within n words), `%` (but not), `!` for word endings.
- **Create the alert**: run the search; **Create alert** (a **WestClip** alert on new documents matching the search; **KeyCite** alerts follow a case's or statute's later history). In the alert's delivery, add the alerts address.
- **Email**: to the addresses entered on the alert.

## Factiva

- **Access**: through the user's institution.
- **Search syntax**: `"exact phrase"`, `and`, `or`, `not`, `near5`, `same` (same paragraph), `hlp=` (headline and lead paragraph), `atleast3 word`, sources and subjects picked from the search form's lists.
- **Create the alert**: run the search; **Create Alert** (from the results page, or **Alerts** in the menu); set **Email** delivery with the alerts address and how often.
- **Email**: to the address entered on the alert. Factiva's links need the institution's access to open.
